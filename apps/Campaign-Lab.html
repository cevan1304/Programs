Metric reference
CTR · Click-through rateClicks ÷ impressions × 100
CPC · Cost per clickSpend ÷ clicks
CPM · Cost per 1,000 impressionsSpend ÷ impressions × 1,000
CPA · Cost per acquisitionSpend ÷ attributed conversions
ROAS · Return on ad spendAttributed revenue ÷ spend
CVR · Conversion rateConversions ÷ specified click or visit base × 100
CPV · Cost per viewSpend ÷ counted video views
